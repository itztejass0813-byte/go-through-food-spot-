Your Fav Food Spot — a Multi-Armed Bandit game

In front of you are 4 food spots: McDonald's, Burger King, Leon Grill, and Taco Bell. Each food spot has a hidden reward that you don't know at the beginning.

You have 30 visits. Each time you choose a food spot, you receive a reward. Your goal is to maximize your total reward.

Every visit is a choice: explore a food spot you haven't learned much about, or exploit a food spot that has given you good rewards so far.

Choose your food spot and see what happens! 🍔
